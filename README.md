# Michael Bauer Smith Sr.

The family of Michael Bauer Smith Sr. (born 1949, Terre Haute, Indiana), from the records: his parents Ralph Warren Smith and Patricia Antoinette Bauer, and the Smith, Hults, Killen, Bauer and Beam lines of Vigo County, Indiana, back to Kentucky, Ohio and Frauendorf in Lower Austria. Compiled September 2026 by Grace Goodwin.
